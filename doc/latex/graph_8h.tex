Creates a random regular graph of {\itshape N} nodes with uniform connectivity {\itshape d}. 

Algorithm 1 in \mbox{[}St\+W99\mbox{]}. Draws a random graph of size {\itshape N} and uniform degree {\itshape d} from an almost uniform probability distribution over these graphs (which becomes uniform in the limit that {\itshape d} is small and {\itshape N} goes to infinity).
