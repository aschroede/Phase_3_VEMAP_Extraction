\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Represents a vector with entries of type {\itshape T}. 

It is simply a {\ttfamily std\+::vector}\texorpdfstring{$<$}{<}{\itshape T}\texorpdfstring{$>$}{>} with an interface designed for dealing with probability mass functions.

It is mainly used for representing measures on a finite outcome space, for example, the probability distribution of a discrete random variable. However, entries are not necessarily non-\/negative; it is also used to represent logarithms of probability mass functions.


\begin{DoxyTemplParams}{Template Parameters}
{\em T} & Should be a scalar that is castable from and to \doxylink{util_8h_ae7d0472fdc89a8635825d01940e91cbf}{dai\+::\+Real} and should support elementary arithmetic operations. \\
